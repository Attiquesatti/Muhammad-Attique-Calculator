# Salaried income tax slabs, Pakistan, FY 2026-27

Source to cite: Finance Act 2026 (FBR), effective 1 July 2026.
Cross-checked against: https://www.mytaxcalculator.pk/
Status: salaried rates verified against FBR document: [YES / NO - update this]

Annual taxable income (Rs.) -> annual tax
- Up to 600,000 -> 0
- 600,001 to 1,200,000 -> 1% of the amount exceeding 600,000
- 1,200,001 to 2,200,000 -> 6,000 + 11% of the amount exceeding 1,200,000
- 2,200,001 to 3,200,000 -> 116,000 + 20% of the amount exceeding 2,200,000
- 3,200,001 to 4,100,000 -> 316,000 + 25% of the amount exceeding 3,200,000
- 4,100,001 to 5,600,000 -> 541,000 + 29% of the amount exceeding 4,100,000
- 5,600,001 to 7,000,000 -> 976,000 + 32% of the amount exceeding 5,600,000
- Above 7,000,000 -> 1,424,000 + 35% of the amount exceeding 7,000,000

Notes
- The 9% surcharge on salaried income above Rs 10 million is abolished; do not apply it.
- Annual taxable income = monthly salary x 12.

# Non-salaried (business / sole proprietor) income tax slabs, FY 2026-27

Source: mytaxcalculator.pk/business-tax-calculator (cites Finance Act 2026 / First Schedule, Income Tax Ordinance 2001).
Cross-checked independently: top rate 45% and surcharge for non-salaried. Middle rates not independently verified.

Annual taxable income = net business PROFIT (sales minus allowed expenses), not sales.

- Up to 600,000 -> 0
- 600,001 to 1,200,000 -> 15% of the amount exceeding 600,000
- 1,200,001 to 1,600,000 -> 90,000 + 20% of the amount exceeding 1,200,000
- 1,600,001 to 3,200,000 -> 170,000 + 30% of the amount exceeding 1,600,000
- 3,200,001 to 5,600,000 -> 650,000 + 40% of the amount exceeding 3,200,000
- Above 5,600,000 -> 1,610,000 + 45% of the amount exceeding 5,600,000

Surcharge: if taxable income is above Rs 10,000,000, add 10% of the tax (not of the income).
Assumption: business income only (no salary), sole proprietor. This table is separate from the salaried table and must never be mixed with it.
